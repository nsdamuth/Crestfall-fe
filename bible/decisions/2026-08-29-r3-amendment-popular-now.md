# DECISION: R3 amended, the top shelf displays Popular now (S2)

Date: 29 Aug 2026. Gate: other (Sol round 1 response ruling).
Decider: Brian. Status: RULED.

## What changed and why

R3 (29 Aug 2026, Home PRD plan approval) ruled the top shelf name
"Top rated". Sol's round 1 finding F-004 showed the name has no
truthful ranking behind it: the current order is a recommendation
blend (featured, canon, weighted engagement, recency), and the save
term in the FE formula contributes zero because the Services
community summary does not supply saves on this path.

Ruling S2, on that evidence: the shelf displays "Popular now" until
a real rating metric exists. "Top rated" is reserved for that future
metric via a flagged CR-029 amendment (defining inputs, weights, tie
breakers, canon and featured treatment, and whether saves
participate; if they do, Services must expose them on this path).

This amends R3 for the top shelf only. The rest of R3 stands:
"Recently added" remains the second shelf's name, now explicitly
ordered by creation time (F-003 fix).
