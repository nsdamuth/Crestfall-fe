## title

Re-ground Home as the Play group's guidepost: land, pick up the story
you were in with one tap, and step into any section of Crestfall
without Home ever holding content of its own.

## problem

Two pains, one page.

For the player: landing in Crestfall today gives no single surface
that says "here is where you left off" and "here is everywhere you
can go". A returning player has to remember and navigate; a new
player has to guess.

For the project: a Home page already exists and already reads live
data, but it drifted during the 29 Aug convergence. Its closing
banner sold the Studio instead of Stories (breaking the ruled journey
loop), its shelf names changed without a ruling, a ruled filter
control never appeared, and the function map still describes the page
as a fixture mockup. Evidence: the convergence audit
(docs/reviews/FE-CONVERGENCE-AUDIT-2026-08.md) and the row-by-row
echo in this PRD. The R1 to R4 rulings above settle which Home is the
real one; this PRD is the record.

## outcome

After this ships, a player who lands on Home resumes their most
recent Story in one tap, sees all eight other sections one tap away,
and skims four short shelves of work worth playing. Home routes
outward every time; it never becomes a feed or a dashboard.

## user_stories (P0 only)

1. As a returning player with a Story in progress, I land on Home
   and continue that Story in one tap.
2. As a player with nothing in progress, I land on Home and get a
   clear invitation to start, never a blank or apologetic page.
3. As a player, I reach any of the other eight sections from Home in
   one tap.
4. As a player, I skim four short shelves (Popular now, Recently
   added, From the community, Creators to follow), and I can heart,
   save, or follow directly from a shelf and trust it stuck.
5. As a creator, I narrow the three creation shelves to just my own
   work (or a visibility) and Home remembers that choice next visit.
6. As the design authority, I look at Home and every surface, word,
   and color obeys the ruled law, and every control's wiring is
   recorded in the ledger.

## key_flows_and_edge_cases (Tier B)

- Warm landing: AC1. Cold landing: AC2.
- Several items in progress: the merged banner shows only the most
  recent; a strip can show several. This difference is part of what
  the C2 render sitting weighs; the Stories page remains the full
  in-progress list either way.
- Partial load failure: each feed fails independently and the page
  renders what loaded, with the existing quiet warning strip for
  what did not. Home never renders blank because one shelf failed.
- Filter with no matches: a narrowed shelf with nothing to show
  renders nothing, same as an empty rail; the filter control itself
  always stays visible so the player can widen it again.
- Muted creators are excluded from all five feeds (CR-028)
  server-side once both CRs are live. Contract warning
  (ACCEPTED-DEFERRED, F-008): the current public discovery path is
  actorless, so server-side per-account mute exclusion is
  impossible until discovery becomes actor-aware; CR-028 and
  CR-029 carry this warning and cannot claim server-side mute
  exclusion before that lands.
- Signed-out visitor: RULED S3 (closes F-007): Home requires
  sign-in under the current /studio/** authority (the parent
  studio layout returns the signed-out gate for every /studio/**
  address, so /studio/v2/home never decides this alone). A public
  Home for signed-out visitors is a separate future route and
  chassis decision, out of this PRD.
- Long titles, missing art: kit card and banner contracts already
  carry truncation and fallback. Correction (F-006): the

Authority read for this table (current source, this session):
`lib/server/creations/constants.js` in the converged Crestfall repo
(CREATION_VISIBILITIES: PRIVATE, UNLISTED, PUBLIC; CREATION_STATUSES:
DRAFT, IN_REVIEW, APPROVED, REJECTED, ARCHIVED;
CREATION_CANON_STATUSES: NONE, CANDIDATE, COMPATIBLE, OFFICIAL), the
publishing section ViewModel (only OFFICIAL is treated as canon; a

locally inspectable (the community route proxies to
/v1/community/creations); everything resting on it is marked OPEN
below, never assumed.

### The projection, evaluated top to bottom, first match wins

| Precedence | Backend facts | Home display bucket |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | canonStatus = OFFICIAL (any visibility, any status) | CANON, exclusive: never also PUBLIC (RULED S1) |
| 2 | canonStatus in {NONE, CANDIDATE, COMPATIBLE} and visibility = PUBLIC and status = APPROVED | PUBLIC |
| 3 | same canonStatus set, visibility = UNLISTED (any status, including the APPROVED link-share state and the moved-to-internal-editing DRAFT state) | INTERNAL (the unlisted link-share state, RULED S1) |
| 4 | same canonStatus set, visibility = PRIVATE (any status) | PRIVATE |
| OPEN | visibility = PUBLIC with status not APPROVED (DRAFT, IN_REVIEW, REJECTED, ARCHIVED) | OPEN for Sol round 2: the transitions read this session never produce these combinations, but their absence is unproven; if any exists in live data, its bucket needs a ruling, not a guess |

Notes held deterministic by the rulings:

- CANDIDATE and COMPATIBLE are not canon: they fall through to the
  visibility rows above, matching the chassis code where only
  OFFICIAL is treated specially. Whether either deserves any Home
  badge or treatment is OPEN for Sol round 2 (O3 below); the bucket
  itself is not open.
- "Just mine" filters on ownership alone and combines with any
  bucket (RULED S1). Mine plus Public excludes the user's own canon
  work; that work answers only to Mine plus Canon, because Canon is
  exclusive.

### The candidate set for the three creation shelves (answers F-009)

The shelves draw from the union of both collections Home already
receives, never from the community list alone:

1. Union ownedCreations and communityCreations.
2. Deduplicate by creation id; the owned copy wins (RULED S1), so
   the user's own public work appears once, with its ownership
   intact.
3. Derive per item: ownership (mine when the id is in
   ownedCreations, otherwise others) and the display bucket per the
   projection table.
4. Apply the CR-030 filter: ownership first, then bucket. Filtering
   always precedes ranking (RULED S1).
5. Rank and project the surviving items into the shelves.

### OPEN for Sol round 2 (confirmations, not guesses)

- O1: the exact eligibility predicate of the Services

- O2: whether visibility PUBLIC ever coexists with a status other
  than APPROVED in live data (the OPEN row above).
- O3: whether canonStatus CANDIDATE or COMPATIBLE carries any Home
  display consequence beyond falling through to its visibility
  bucket.
- O4: whether ARCHIVED items are eligible for the shelves at all
  (their bucket is defined above; their eligibility is a product
  and Services question).

## faq (working backwards)

Q: I opened Home and my story was right there; is that always the
top thing?
A: Yes. If anything of yours is in progress, continuing it is the
first thing Home offers. What that surface looks like is being
chosen from renders, but its position is settled.

Q: Why does Home not show me everything new on the platform?
A: Home is a guidepost. Community is the catalog, Stories is your
playroom, and Home's job is to get you to either in one tap. Its
shelves are short teasers, not the library.

Q: Where did the big Studio banner at the bottom go?
A: The journey loop sends you from Home to Stories; each page hands
you to the next. The Studio has its own door among the eight tiles
and its own stop later in the loop.

Q: Can I trust the shelf names ("Popular now") to mean what they say?
A: Not fully until the real curated feeds land (a flagged backend
item); until then the shelves show honest orderings of what the
platform can already serve, and nothing is faked.
