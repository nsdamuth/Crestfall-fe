# Design rulings, 11 Sep 2026 (Brian)

Six rulings, recorded verbatim.

## R6 Sharp line

Sharp line: only on the primary page eyebrow above the page title. Not
on section headers, cards, forms, modals, or sidebars. Plain dividers
may still break up sections.

## R7 CTA groups

CTA groups: buttons in the same component share one size, right
aligned by default, anchored to opposite sides only where the
component calls for it.

## R8 Modal dimensions

Modal dimensions: one standard width and height, matching the
character modal. Deviation needs a named reason.

## R9 Modal header and divider

Modal header and divider: small gold eyebrow, and the divider is the
gold line faded at both ends, never a white rule.

## R10 Form surfaces

Form surfaces: fields a step darker, dropdowns a step lighter, native
OS selects replaced with the Kit select, nested section borders
dropped for one shared background per distinct section, checkbox in a
brand color.

## R11 Story chat

Story chat: top margin so the surface is not flush with the global
nav.
