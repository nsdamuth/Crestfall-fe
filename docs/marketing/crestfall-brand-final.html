What We Are (and Aren't)

We are

A home for living stories. Free to play, gorgeous to look at, built by people who share in what they make. Romance-forward, grown-up, and safe for brands.

We are not

An adults-only chat site. A coin-hungry casual app. A lonely writing tool. A place where the story costs money.

Voice & Look

Voice: warm, mythic, precise. Our words: story, play, build, remember, world. Never system words with players: persistent, monetize, content, bots, users. Write the way YouTube and Wattpad write (you, everyone, play), never the way a pitch deck writes. Look: obsidian and antiqued gold, a slow gold galaxy behind the name, sharp gold rules, camellia line art between movements, Cormorant Garamond over Alegreya Sans, color from character art rather than UI chrome. Never an em dash.
