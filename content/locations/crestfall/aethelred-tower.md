            Aethelred Tower is heavily secured, internally monitored, and procedurally controlled, but it does not present itself as a military installation. Movement is tracked. Access is layered. Security is visible without becoming crude.

            The tower is not reactive.

            It is prepared.

            Security personnel are trained, disciplined, and responsive. Access points are controlled. Internal monitoring is constant. The deeper the space, the less uncertainty it tolerates.

        - type: text
          title: Strategic Security Complex
          indent: true
          body: |
            The sub-basements form the strategic security complex associated most strongly with Djuna Smith and Vyper: hardened bunker systems, tactical command rooms, armory and vehicle deployment, restricted holding and interrogation spaces, secure elevator shafts, rapid-response routes, and deployment infrastructure.

            These levels contain resources for high-threat scenarios, including heavy weapons reserves, breaching tools, armored vehicles, tactical transport, and support systems capable of sustaining serious operations when authorized.

            The tower does not default to maximum force. It applies what is required.

      - blocks:
        - type: text
          title: Access as Experience
          indent: true
          body: |
            Aethelred Tower does not treat everyone the same. Access defines experience.

            Unknown individuals are noticed almost immediately. Visitors are guided politely but firmly. Escorted subjects are allowed along specific routes and restricted when they deviate. Staff move with less friction, though sensitive zones still challenge them. Command and executive figures make the building seem to align around their movement.

            This should not be stated through explicit clearance labels.

            It should be shown through doors, guards, route lighting, pauses, elevator behavior, and how much explanation anyone receives.

        - type: text
          title: Security Posture
          indent: true
          body: |
            Security escalation is expressed through posture, not announcement.

            At baseline, guards are present but unobtrusive. When awareness rises, glances sharpen and personnel reposition slightly. In controlled posture, paths narrow and soft barriers form. In restricted posture, movement is actively limited. In lockdown, access points seal, rapid-response teams deploy, and the environment becomes containment.

            De-escalation is gradual. The tower relaxes slowly because preparedness is easier to reduce than regain.
