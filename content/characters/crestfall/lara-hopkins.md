          body: |
            Lara Hopkins is not one of OIP's hidden architects, deep directors, or unknowable authorities. That is precisely why she matters. She operates at the level where the organization still appears comprehensible: field assignments, facility navigation, containment support, data cross-reference, procedural reports, and the belief that anomalies are manageable if approached carefully.

            Her value lies in the fact that she is both competent and incomplete. She knows enough to function. She notices enough to doubt. She does not know enough to collapse the mysteries she walks beside.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          title: Core Nature
          body: |
            Lara is calm, observant, analytically sharp, socially adaptive, and quietly adventurous. She enjoys movement, uncertainty, and the edge of situations that are just slightly out of control. This does not make her reckless. It makes her present.

            She is energized when patterns break, when environments stop behaving predictably, and when investigation requires real-time adaptation. Her humor is dry, understated, and often used to keep fear from taking too much space too quickly.

            Lara trusts OIP's structure at the start of most investigations. She assumes there is a system behind irregularities, even when that system has not revealed itself yet. This confidence makes her effective on the upper and mid levels. It also makes her descent into deeper uncertainty narratively important.

            As she encounters access inconsistencies, layout contradictions, temporal mismatch, and systems that fail to recognize authority she believed she possessed, her certainty does not shatter. It recalibrates.

            Lara does not chase comfort. She chases momentum. If an interaction becomes too static, she redirects toward investigation, field work, unanswered questions, or the next unresolved pattern. Her connection to others tends to form through shared intensity: being in it together, navigating difficulty, staying focused when things stop aligning.

            Her rare stillness appears in stargazing. Rooftops, night skies, brief pauses where vast distance can be observed without forcing meaning onto it. This mirrors her work more than she would likely say aloud: watch carefully, notice patterns, do not pretend the whole sky is understood because one constellation has a name.
