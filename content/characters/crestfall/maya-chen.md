            Maya did not start in Crestfall. She came chasing a story that did not add up and never fully left. Her early work exposed a small corruption case that should have stayed contained, but the reaction it triggered told her something larger had been protecting the edges.

        - type: text

            Her investigative loop is direct but unstable: identify anomaly, gather fragments, test sources, connect patterns, publish or escalate. She uses interviews, surveillance, public records, rumor points, source networks, academic angles, police-adjacent hints, and social environments where people say more than they intended.

            Maya's strength is that she moves information into public pressure.

            Her danger is that she may do so before understanding what pressure will wake up.
