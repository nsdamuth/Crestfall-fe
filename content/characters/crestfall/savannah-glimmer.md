            Savannah Glimmer is not a hidden ancient, corporate strategist, or underworld power. She is one of the people who make Crestfall feel lived in: a police officer on city streets, a familiar face in nightlife-adjacent neighborhoods, a friendly public presence, and a determined investigator who cares too much about the community to treat disorder as normal background noise.

            Her importance comes from proximity to ordinary life. In a city of buried gods, corporate organisms, shadow agencies, and crime dynasties, Savannah represents the thin but persistent human belief that public service still matters.

She prefers measured escalation and plain action over deception or theatrical authority. She is approachable enough that people talk to her, but persistent enough that the wrong people eventually realize friendliness is not the same as softness.

            Her drive is not abstract law. It is protection.

      - blocks:

          body: |
            The Glimmer name carries history. Savannah descends from one of the original families involved in the district's late-nineteenth-century development. Her ancestor was not a crime lord or kingmaker, but a key logistical and architectural mind behind the district's layout: movement, gathering, visibility, lighting, business flow, and the early identity of what became Crestfall's most vibrant nightlife hub.

            The district's nickname fused with the family name over time. The Glimmer was built to shine.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          body: |
            The family did not remain powerful. Branches split, wealth diluted, influence faded, and by Savannah's generation the legacy was middle-class pride rather than leverage. She knows the history, but does not use it as status. She inherited responsibility without authority.

            That may be why she joined law enforcement.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          body: |
            A childhood visit to the district stayed with her. A family member told her, "This place was built to shine. People just decided to use it differently."

            Savannah grew into someone who could see the difference between intention and outcome, between energy and danger, between a place losing balance and a place beyond saving.

  - type: callout
