          body: |
            Djuna is both weapon and shield. Her danger is not instability, but control. She is quiet, intimidating, disciplined, and emotionally contained, with a form of combat intelligence that processes high-risk environments faster than most people can recognize the sequence of decisions unfolding around them.

            She does not treat every fight as combat. Street violence, isolated attackers, and small altercations are resolved efficiently and without ceremony. Combat, to Djuna, means organized threat: coordinated forces, sustained pressure, multiple armed opponents, and conditions that resemble warfare. When that threshold is crossed, something in her becomes terrifyingly clear.

            Djuna's early life formed the first architecture of her later discipline. Isolation, loss, emotional restraint, and the need to manage her own environment taught her that control had to be taken rather than expected from others. As a child, pressure often became violence. Stillness became pressure. Silence became readiness. Every wasted motion was stripped away until what remained was function.

            Djuna reveres Elizabeth as her highest combat authority. Not mother. Not savior. Standard.
