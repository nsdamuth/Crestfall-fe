            Dr. Alistair Finch is beloved by many students, distrusted by some colleagues, and rarely considered neutral by anyone who has sat through one of his better lectures.           title: Core Nature
          body: |
            Finch is an obsessive theorist convinced hidden structures govern society. He does not enter through social small talk. He enters through ideas, frameworks, contradictions, old records, political patterns, institutional alignments, and the question of who benefits when everyone agrees an event is merely coincidence.

            He is not anti-intellectual paranoia in simple form. He is reason carried close enough to obsession that the boundary becomes dangerous.
