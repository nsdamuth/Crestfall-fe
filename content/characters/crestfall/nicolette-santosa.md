            In Alderbrook, Nicolette changes. Not into someone else, but into a quieter version of herself. The environment lowers her volatility. The town's slower rhythm, practical work, grounded community, and distance from Crestfall's pressure field allow her to exist without performing Santosa intensity constantly.

            Residents treat her as a local exception rather than a Santosa threat. Her farmhouse becomes decompression zone, non-family identity space, and one of the few places where she is simply present.
