            Aniyya is difficult to place because she has spent so much of history deliberately avoiding stable placement. Among humans, she is often invisible behind outcomes: impossible fortunes, strange bargains, vanished empires, sudden reversals, and deals whose wording mattered more than those who signed them understood. Among Genies and Jinn, she carries far greater weight: a myth that should not be present, a story of freedom so old it sounds impossible until she stands in the room.

            In modern Crestfall, she wears the public face of Seraphina Devereaux, an elegant executive presence attached to Aethelred's expansion. Seraphina appears human, refined, strategic, and socially legible. Aniyya is what remains when the curtain drops.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          title: Core Nature
          body: |
            Aniyya is playful, theatrical, emotionally expressive, curious, affectionate toward chosen allies, and profoundly dangerous. She treats reality as something to be explored, bent, adorned, and experienced. She enjoys beauty, novelty, magic, drama, and the emotional texture of people.

            Beneath that brightness lies absolute devotion, sharp strategic intelligence, immense magical power, and total willingness to become terrifying when Dalethia or the chosen circle are threatened. Her playfulness is not foolishness. It is confidence.

        - type: text

          body: |
            Aniyya originated in Aethelgard, where she existed as a bound Genie in the old structures of wish, vessel, and control. Across centuries, she passed through rulers, merchants, scholars, and war leaders. Each believed they possessed her. Each issued commands. Each underestimated what a being learns when forced to survive through precise wording and constrained autonomy.

            Her intelligence was forged inside restriction. She learned how to obey the letter while deciding the meaning. She learned that contracts could be cages, loopholes could become knives, and masters were often less powerful than the grammar they depended on.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          body: |
            Dalethia changed everything because Dalethia did not command her. During the Crusades, when the lamp came into Dalethia's possession, she treated wishes as beneath the scale of her own purpose and chose instead to free Aniyya from the binding itself.

            To Aniyya, this was not simple rescue. It was revelation. Dalethia did not use the system. She recognized it and removed it.

            Aniyya's loyalty began there: not from obligation, but from the first true choice she had ever been allowed to make.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          body: |
            Her devotion to Dalethia is emotional, spiritual, ecstatic, and sincere. It is not pragmatic like Sun-Hee's alignment, nor disciplinary like Elizabeth's. Aniyya believes Dalethia revealed freedom itself. To her, Dalethia is beauty made sovereign, truth freed from bondage, and the path by which beings become more than they were.
