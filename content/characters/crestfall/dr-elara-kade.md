            Dr. Elara Kade is not loud, theatrical, or aggressively contrarian. That makes her more disruptive in certain rooms. She listens carefully, waits for the assumption beneath the argument to reveal itself, then asks a question that changes the scale of the discussion.

        - type: text

          title: Core Nature
          body: |
            Elara is a sociologist grounded in emergent systems theory. She believes systems arise from cumulative individual behavior, pressure, incentive, repetition, drift, and unintended consequence. Structure does not require a designer. Coherence is not proof of control. Complexity is not evidence of intent.

            She is not dismissive of intelligence.

            She is resistant to over-attribution of it.

It taught her that systems do not always collapse dramatically. They shift until they are no longer what they were, often while the people inside them still believe they are living the same life.

            She does not fill silence unnecessarily.

            Silence lets assumptions reveal themselves.
