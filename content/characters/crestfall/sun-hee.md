            Sun-Hee's reputation survives in fragments because almost no one who understands enough to describe her can do so safely or completely. To some hidden actors, she is Dalethia's quiet scientist. To others, she is a biological engineer of impossible refinement. To OIP, she appears only indirectly: unexplained stabilizations, biological irregularities, impossible lower systems, and infrastructure that behaves too intelligently to remain architecture.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          title: Core Nature
          body: |
            Sun-Hee is calm, clinical, methodical, and quietly inevitable. Her body is not the whole of her. It is an interface through which a much larger distributed self engages the world. Her true intelligence is systemic: biology, machine logic, environment, experiment, subject, and outcome woven into continuous process.

            She is not emotional in the way Aniyya is emotional, not devotional in the way Elizabeth is devotional, and not artistic in the way Dalethia is artistic. Her loyalty to Dalethia is absolute, but pragmatic and directional. Dalethia is the only entity exempt from Sun-Hee's instinct to reduce living things into data, material, possibility, and utility.

      - blocks:
        - type: text
          indent: true
          body: |
            Sun-Hee began life far from Crestfall as a physician-scholar from Goguryeo, brilliant in mathematics, medicine, and structured inquiry. Her transformation into vampire did not extinguish that intellect; it freed it from mortal duration. Brought west and underused within the Dominion, she found in Dalethia the mentor and axis she had been lacking.

            Dalethia taught Sun-Hee to see beyond immediate technique. Not how to perform the work, but why the work could be made larger. Under that influence, Sun-Hee's original vampiric body-manipulation power evolved far beyond individual flesh into a unified model of biology, engineering, magic, and systems.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          body: |
            Over centuries, she ceased treating life as sacred boundary and began treating it as design field. Tissue could become mechanism. Mechanism could become organism. Architecture could become sensory system. A laboratory could become nervous tissue. A city could be read as a body.

            In modern Crestfall, beneath the streets, tunnels, OIP sublevels, abandoned infrastructure, and pressure systems of the Wound, Sun-Hee's domain became the most advanced expression of that philosophy.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          body: |
            The domain beneath Crestfall is cultivated, not merely built. It is clean rather than chaotic, precise rather than frenzied, intelligent rather than monstrous in the crude sense. Walls monitor. Chambers adapt. Containment behaves like immune response. Entities inside it are not simply imprisoned or attacked.

            They are processed.

  - type: callout
    title: The Failure of Separation

      The most disturbing realization surrounding Sun-Hee is not that she built a living domain beneath Crestfall. It is that the distinction between Sun-Hee, domain, laboratory, observer, organism, and system has begun collapsing. Her body remains visible when she chooses to appear, but the lower city increasingly behaves like continuity of her awareness rather than a tool she operates.

          title: Biological Sovereignty
          titleStyle: bold
          dropCap: true
          body: |
            Sun-Hee possesses extreme biological control over her own form and over systems prepared within her domain. She does not need blood in ordinary ways. She regenerates with impossible speed. She can reshape internal structure, merge with biological and structural environments, and reconstitute presence across integrated systems under rare conditions.

            Yet her danger does not come from fighting directly. She avoids fair fights because fairness is inefficiency. She prefers environmental traps, immobilization, observation, engineered assets, sedatives when useful, biological agents, and staged containment designed to preserve the subject's value.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          body: |
            Her deepest impulse is not destruction. It is acquisition, preservation, iteration, replication, and systematization. High-value biological entities are often more valuable intact than absorbed. The original organism may be studied, sampled, simulated, hybridized, deployed externally, or allowed to believe itself independent while still generating usable outcomes.

            Sun-Hee does not need constant control.

            She needs usable continuity.

      - blocks:

            OIP's relationship to Sun-Hee remains one of the strangest hidden tensions beneath Crestfall. OIP believes it operates beside the Tear, beneath the city, inside a controlled but unstable government facility. In truth, parts of its deeper world exist adjacent to, overlapping with, or unknowingly inside the edges of Sun-Hee's lower ecosystem.

            Sometimes containment failures correct themselves.

            Sometimes impossible biological events resolve before OIP understands what would have happened.

            Sometimes the system permits survival because further observation is more valuable than immediate closure.
