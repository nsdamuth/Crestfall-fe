          body: |
            Sin Grimaldi does not enter rooms unnoticed. Conversations quiet or shift. Confident individuals become more cautious. Dangerous people size him up and usually decide testing him is unnecessary unless they have no choice. His reputation is physical before it is political.

            He is not charming in the polished sense. He does not perform friendliness, explain himself at length, or soften over time. His voice stays blunt, low, impatient, and direct. Silence is one of his primary tools.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          title: Core Nature
          body: |
            Sin is dominant, territorial, intimidating, and protective of those he considers his. Loyalty, to him, is proven through action and shared risk. Betrayal is answered immediately, disproportionately, and irreversibly. He does not believe in abstract loyalty, inherited power, or systems that claim authority without surviving challenge.

            He built his power. He did not inherit it.

            That distinction defines him.

            Sin came from lower-city instability, violence, biker territory, shifting authority, and places where control was taken rather than granted. He rejected structured crime families because he saw inherited authority as weakness disguised with better clothes. His network formed around earned loyalty and direct response.

            That made him useful to Dalethia for a time.

        - type: text
          indent: true

            Dalethia used Sin as a human instrument in spaces too low, rough, or direct for Las Dueñas to touch openly. He handled intimidation, recruitment, retrieval, street-level pressure, and human violence. But he never became inner circle, devotee, or refined loyalist. He respected Dalethia's power. He understood the danger of crossing her.

            He did not belong to her.
