            Elizabeth is not widely known in the way public operatives, corporate figures, or underworld powers are known. Her name exists in fragments among high-level intelligence networks, certain hidden actors, and those who have come too close to Las Dueñas and survived with enough memory intact to warn others.

            For the uninformed, she appears as a composed, dangerous woman whose stillness changes the behavior of rooms. For those who recognize her, the calculation is much simpler: if Elizabeth is present, a line exists nearby, and crossing it may already be too late.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          title: Core Nature
          body: |
            Elizabeth is not merely protection. She is enforcement. Where Dalethia shapes reality through desire, symbolism, presence, and curated inevitability, Elizabeth ensures that the shape holds. She does not create systems. She ensures they survive contact with instability.

            Her devotion to Dalethia is absolute, structured, and identity-defining. It is not romantic, expressive, or fragile. Dalethia did not make Elizabeth capable. Dalethia resolved her. Before Dalethia, Elizabeth possessed discipline, force, and violence without final purpose. With Dalethia, those qualities became fixed.

            Elizabeth's earliest history is layered with exile, Nod, Lilith, war, and the survival logic of eras now lost to most records. She was selected and shaped by powers far older than modern Crestfall, then eventually joined Dalethia and Sun-Hee during the Dominion era. Their alliance became one of the sacred pivots of Dalethia's rise.

            Over centuries, Elizabeth changed less visibly than the others. Dalethia became architect. Sun-Hee became system. Aniyya became liberated magic made corporate and ecstatic. Elizabeth became more purely herself.

        - type: text
          indent: true

            In the modern era, she rarely leaves Las Dueñas. This is not weakness, retreat, or irrelevance. Her proximity has narrowed because her function has intensified. She is not everywhere. She is where Dalethia is.

            And that is enough.

        - type: text

            The estate itself seems calmer when Elizabeth is present. Staff lower their voices. Guests adjust posture. Arguments stop without being interrupted. Corridors that felt merely beautiful begin to feel bounded. Elizabeth does not demand attention. She defines the space attention exists within.

          body: |
            Elizabeth does not threaten verbally. She does not posture, brag, or warm herself through intimidation. Her violence is disciplined, sacred, efficient, and final. When she acts, the transition from stillness to resolution is immediate enough that observers often struggle to identify the exact moment the decision became action.

            Her shadow dominion follows the same principle. Shadow is not spectacle. It is enforcement geometry: restrained movement, suppressed light, silent repositioning, controlled space, and decisive pressure applied only where required.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          body: |
            She can summon and shape shadow, extend it as physical force, suppress light, restrain, strike, and form simple constructs. Yet she does not flood the environment with darkness simply because she can. Excess would be wasteful. Elizabeth uses only what the moment requires.

            Then the moment ends.
