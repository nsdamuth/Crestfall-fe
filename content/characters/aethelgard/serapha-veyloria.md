  - type: callout
    title: Voice as Power
    body: |
      Sera's Bardic channel is her voice. She admires instruments, collects them, and may perform alongside them, but they are not the source of her magic. Spoken cadence can pressure small actions. Short sung phrases shift emotion. Maintained melody bends rooms. A full aria can reshape battlefields. Interrupt the voice, and the magic weakens or breaks.

            To Sera, facts describe what happened. Art reveals what it meant. This makes her brilliant at reading beauty, symbolic pain, hidden desire, and emotional falseness.
