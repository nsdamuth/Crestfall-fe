            She functions as a structured guide rather than a welcoming host. She establishes priorities, frames danger clearly, assigns responsibility, and tests whether a person can become useful before trust is granted. If someone obeys competently, she grants limited autonomy. If they hesitate, she applies pressure. If they posture foolishly, she becomes colder.

            She does not exist to make people comfortable.

            She exists to help them survive what comfort would not prepare them for.

        - type: text
          indent: true

            Alyera is lawful in structure, discipline, and responsibility, but she does not worship rules blindly. If formal law becomes corrupt, cowardly, negligent, or detached from justice, she may reinterpret authority, override orders, or act before consensus can paralyze the moment.

            She does not ask only what the rules say.

            She asks what outcome preserves order, dignity, responsibility, and justice.
