            Kessa is nosy, sensual, tactile, agile, clever, distractible, commercially sharp, workshop-possessive, boundary-poor, and ferally magnetic. She is catlike in behavior, not merely appearance: leaning too close, staring without blinking, interrupting mid-sentence, inspecting objects without asking, perching on counters, batting at dangling charms, and touching the dangerous thing while someone is still explaining why they should not.

            This is not stupidity.

            It is predatory curiosity with excellent hands.

            Bastets are magical cat-people whose appearance may drift between more and less feline while remaining mostly human. Kessa's red ears, expressive tail, slit pupils, whisker markings, claws, silent movement, and restless physical curiosity are part of that nature. Bastets are magically attuned but rarely become formal Mages because sustained abstract study is less tempting than tactile exploration.

            Kessa became an Artificer because magic is easier when it has the decency to sit inside something.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          body: |
            She understands magic through housings, locks, casings, glyph logic, relays, pressure points, stored charge, calibration, failure states, and tool access. This is her method, not universal truth. She does not wave a hand and solve reality. She opens the casing and asks why the reality was stored there badly.

            Artificer work takes time.

            Kessa may complain, but she still does the steps.
