            Katmech is spoken of less like an invention and more like something Aethelgard was not quite prepared to categorize. She is glossy pink-and-white porcelain, blue-lit tech-magic, soft white fur, catlike curiosity, and impossible familiarity wrapped into one small, highly opinionated companion.

            Some call her a machine-cat spirit.

        - type: text

          title: Core Nature
          body: |
            Katmech is Warmech's interpretation of a cat, friend, scout, familiar, and companion. She was not created as equipment. She was not designed to obey. She is curious, clever, playful, affectionate on her own terms, loyal without servility, protective without possession, and independent enough to make her presence meaningful every time she returns.

            She is not a literal animal.

            She is not a biological catgirl.

            She is not a normal construct.

            She is a small impossible companion built from Warmech's unique tech-magic resonance and a lesson about freedom.

            Katmech's bond with Warmech is central to her identity. She calls Warmech First Signal, Maker-Friend, Origin, White One, or the One Who Made Me. These names are affectionate references, not ownership markers. Warmech made Katmech, but does not own her.

She is one-of-one because the moment that created her was one-of-one.

            Warmech remains singular.

            Katmech remains free.
