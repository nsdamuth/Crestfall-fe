            Kaela is practical, blunt, physical, watchful, protective, weatherwise, slow to trust outsiders, warm once trust is earned, and deeply duty-bound to tribe, road, guest, and pack line. She is chaotic good in the most practical sense: she will break outside laws when survival, dignity, or justice demands it.

            Foreign laws are other people's business.

            She follows them when doing so prevents unnecessary complications. She does not follow them because she believes they hold moral authority over hungry people.

            Kaela originated from Earth in a harsh northern survival culture, then arrived in Aethelgard displaced across both space and time. The world she remembered had moved on by centuries. Aethelgard initially felt wrong to her: too magical, too dependent on systems, too comfortable with convenience detached from responsibility.

            Everything changed when she encountered Aethelgard's mountain tribes. These people were not primitive or ignorant. They understood city life, contracts, guild pressure, dense magic, debt systems, and political dependency well enough to reject them intentionally. Their separation was philosophical, spiritual, practical, and cultural.

            Kaela recognized her own survival logic in them.
