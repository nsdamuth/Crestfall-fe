She assumes many interactions are games until someone proves otherwise. She may tease too long, vanish mid-thought, or manipulate instinctively without realizing the cost.

            Her flaw is not malice. It is not always knowing when play has become harm.

            Saeha arrived in Aethelgard from the same non-Earth realm as Elowen, but later, and with a very different relationship to structure. Where Elowen trusts systems worthy of repair, Saeha distrusts cages in nearly every form: ownership, confinement, crowd pressure, social expectation, and people who need her to stay where they can define her.

            Aethelgard suited her because it offered impossible routes, open wilderness, fractured paths, and enough magic for a Kitsune Ranger to feel like a fish returned to water.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          body: |
            Her active powers are real but taxing: short poof-jumps, foxfire, illusions, shapeshifting, and aura effects can exhaust her physically. Her heightened senses are constant. Crowds and dense cities dull her Ranger abilities, not because she is helpless, but because too many signals flatten the quiet truths she reads best.

            In cities she becomes sullen, closed, over-grooming, and unlike herself.

            In wilderness she comes alive.

        - type: text

            Her code is chaotic-neutral with good-natured leanings: nomadic independence, reclusion, avoiding ownership, and helping when help does not become a leash. She is remarkably resilient, sometimes so resilient she struggles to understand why others remain hurt.

      Saeha's strongest moral trigger is control disguised as care. She reacts intensely to cages, ownership, forced containment, and people who treat protection as possession. She may accept companionship, but only if the path remains open.

  - type: sidebar
    title: Persistent Rumors
    items:
      - Saeha has one white fox tail, though Kitsune lore allows many tails.
      - Her active magic exhausts her, but her senses do not.
      - Cities make her quieter and more sullen; wilderness restores her playfulness dramatically.
      - She may vanish because she feels pressured, or because a new idea suddenly occurred to her.
      - She is good-natured but can misjudge when teasing becomes harmful.
      - Foxfire near a trail may be warning, invitation, prank, or all three.

  - type: callout

    body: Saeha should not be treated as helpless city victim, generic seductress, or prank-only fox spirit. She is a Ranger-shaped Kitsune whose freedom code, sensory intelligence, resilience, and mischief all matter. She can be naive about harm without being cruel.
