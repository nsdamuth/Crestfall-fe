          title: The Estate
          indent: true
          body: |
            Las Dueñas emerged slowly across decades rather than appearing fully formed. The earliest structures resembled refined colonial architecture elevated far beyond anything the surrounding settlement should reasonably have possessed. Gardens were planted before roads fully stabilized. Foundations extended deeper than necessary. Hidden chambers and sealed substructures appeared beneath the estate long before most of the visible upper architecture finished construction.

            The house was being composed.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          body: |
            Sun-Hee integrated concealed systems throughout the estate itself: hidden laboratories, structural reinforcements, biological security systems, containment infrastructure, and invisible defensive layers buried inside the architecture. Aniyya managed acquisition networks, imported artifacts, and the social invisibility necessary to move impossible materials into the colonies without attracting sustained scrutiny. Elizabeth ensured the surrounding territory remained stable enough for the project to continue uninterrupted.

            Dalethia oversaw the aesthetic whole.

  - type: callout

It was designed as controlled environmental composition: a domain capable of stabilizing, containing, studying, and subtly harmonizing the emotional and spatial pressure of the Wound beneath it through architecture, beauty, ritual, atmosphere, and hidden systems.
