            As Crestfall accelerated into the late twentieth century, Sun-Hee withdrew from visible society more completely than perhaps any other surviving ancient within the city. While Dalethia retreated upward into symbolic distance and environmental authorship through Las Dueñas, Sun-Hee moved in the opposite direction entirely.

            She descended beneath the city.

          indent: true
          body: |
            At first, the withdrawal resembled practical isolation: hidden laboratories, buried containment chambers, forgotten tunnels, abandoned infrastructure, sealed medical facilities, and restricted maintenance networks beneath Crestfall's expanding urban grid. OIP initially interpreted the scattered evidence this way as well.

            They underestimated the scale catastrophically.

            Over decades, the hidden systems beneath Crestfall stopped resembling conventional laboratories entirely. The deeper structures became cultivated rather than constructed: smooth semi-organic walls without seams, living membrane chambers embedded into architecture, biological-mechanical conduits woven through forgotten infrastructure, subterranean growth systems extending beyond mapped limits, and environmental responses occurring without visible command.

            The underground evolved into ecosystem.

        - type: text

            Sun-Hee no longer approached biology, engineering, medicine, architecture, and computation as separate disciplines. Inside the domain beneath Crestfall, these systems converged into unified process. Walls monitored stress biologically. Ventilation systems functioned like lungs. Structural supports carried sensory feedback like nerves. Entire chambers adapted to emotional, environmental, and metaphysical conditions automatically.

            The domain learned continuously.

          title: The Distributed Self
          indent: true
          body: |
            By the late twentieth century, a small number of hidden observers began reaching disturbing conclusions regarding Sun-Hee herself. Her physical body still existed and remained active within the lower systems beneath Crestfall. Yet increasingly, encounters with her created impossible continuity problems: environmental responses occurring before visible commands, simultaneous system awareness across multiple locations, structural adaptation without communication delay, and the growing inability to distinguish where Sun-Hee ended and the domain began.

            Her body became interface rather than limitation.

          titleStyle: bold

            OIP unknowingly evolved inside the edges of Sun-Hee's ecosystem for decades without fully comprehending the relationship. Researchers believed they were studying environmental anomalies, spatial instability, epistemic drift, biological irregularity, and containment architecture beneath Crestfall. In reality, portions of the lower infrastructure had already become integrated into a distributed intelligence system operating beyond ordinary scientific categories.

            OIP was not alone underground.

        - type: text
          indent: true
          body: |
            Yet Sun-Hee did not treat the organization purely as enemy. She recognized OIP as humanity's first serious attempt to operationalize uncertainty itself. The agency fascinated her intellectually. Certain containment failures resolved mysteriously because she intervened indirectly. Certain deeper descents survived only because the lower systems allowed survival conditions temporarily.

            The organization interpreted these events as luck.

            Sun-Hee preferred that interpretation.

            Sun-Hee's domain felt processed.
